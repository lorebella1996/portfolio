# Dataset Extension Methodology

**Sales Dashboard (2010–2025)**

## Overview

The original dataset (*100 Sales Records*, 2010–2017) was extended to **2025** to enable **multi-year trend analysis** and realistic dashboard insights.
The extension follows **business-driven assumptions** and preserves the structure and patterns of the original data.

---

## Original Dataset

* **Records**: 100 sales transactions
* **Period**: 2010–2017
* **Scope**:

  * 7 geographic regions
  * 12 product categories
  * Online / Offline sales channels

Key patterns (prices, regions, products) were analyzed and used as a baseline.

---

## Extension Logic

The dataset was expanded with **synthetic but controlled data** using the following assumptions:

* **E-commerce growth**: gradual increase of online sales share after 2017
* **Price inflation**: ~3% annual increase
* **COVID impact**:

  * 2020: temporary volume decrease
  * 2021: recovery rebound
* **Volumes**: realistic variability within historical ranges

Random variation was applied to avoid artificial uniformity.

---

## Data Generation Approach

For each new record:

* Regions, products and channels follow original distributions
* Prices are adjusted for inflation and market effects
* Units sold vary within controlled bounds
* Revenue and profit are calculated consistently

The process prioritizes **coherence and realism**, not prediction.

---

## Validation

* Same regions and product categories as original dataset
* No missing or inconsistent values
* Financial metrics within realistic ranges
* Overall trends aligned with business expectations

**Final dataset**:

* 340 records
* 15-year coverage (2010–2025)

---

## Assumptions & Limits

* Simplified trends (no seasonality or competition effects)
* Stable product portfolio and geography
* Dataset designed for **analysis and visualization**, not forecasting
