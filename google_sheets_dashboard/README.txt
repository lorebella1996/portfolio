# Sales Dashboard – Google Sheets

**Junior Data Analyst Portfolio Project**

## Project Overview

Sales performance dashboard built in **Google Sheets** to analyze **Revenue, Profit, Orders and Sales Channels** over a **15-year period (2010–2025)**.
The project simulates a real business environment and focuses on **clear KPIs, trends and decision-oriented insights**.

---

## Data

* **Base dataset**: *100 Sales Records* (public dataset, GitHub)
* **Extended timeframe**: 2010–2025
* **Final size**: 340 sales records
* **Scope**:

  * 7 global regions
  * 12 product categories
  * Online vs Offline sales channels

The dataset was extended to allow **multi-year trend analysis** and realistic business scenarios (e-commerce growth, market expansion).

---

## Dashboard Content

The dashboard is designed as an **executive-ready view** and includes:

* **Core KPIs**: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Orders
* **Time trends**: Revenue and profit evolution (2010–2025)
* **Product analysis**: Revenue vs Profitability by category
* **Channel analysis**: Online vs Offline sales mix evolution
* **Geographic view**: Revenue distribution by region
* **Key Insights**: concise, business-oriented takeaways
